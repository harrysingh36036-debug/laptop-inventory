Four disciplines, one coordinated drawing set

Every system is designed by the same in-house team, against the same model, so the duct, the conduit and the pipe never fight for the same metre of space.

HV

Heating, Ventilation & Air-Conditioning

Full professional consultancy across Heating, Ventilation, Air-Conditioning, Building Management System and Energy Conservation — for shopping complexes, offices, commercial projects, hotels, hospitals, data centres, pharma laboratories and residential projects.

  • Cooling & ventilation load design
  • Clean-room & lab air systems
  • Energy modelling & retrofits
EL

Electrical Engineering

External electrical installation including sub-station and internal electrical, emergency power generation, vertical transportation (elevators & escalators), telephone, master-antenna television, fire alarm & detection, data networking and UPS systems.

  • Sub-station & internal wiring
  • Emergency power & UPS
  • Fire alarm, data & ELV systems
PF

Plumbing & Fire Fighting

Internal cold & hot water supply, soil & waste water disposal, external sewage, storm-water drainage and water-supply fixtures & fittings — plus laundries, kitchens, toilets and low-cost sanitation. Fire protection: hydrant, sprinkler, extinguishers and clean-agent suppression with storage calculation.

  • Cold, hot & sanitary water systems
  • Hydrant & sprinkler networks
  • Clean-agent suppression
BM

Building Management System

Building automation, fire detection & alarm, public address, access control and closed-circuit television security — tying mechanical and electrical assets into one controllable, observable building.

  • BMS & automation
  • Fire detection & alarm
  • CCTV, PA & access control

An MEP firm led by engineering, not overhead

AG Design Consultant & Associates was founded by Mr. Dr. A G Nasim after more than 15 years as a design engineer and associate in reputed firms.

In that time the practice has worked on more than 100 projects — commercial complexes, hospitals, hotels, laboratories and industrial buildings — for developers, PWD, CPWD, MES, EPIL, HSCC, IREO, PIONEER, UINTECH, M3M, Ghaziabad Nagar Nigam and the Noida Metro Rail Corporation, plus overseas work in Egypt, Dubai, Nepal and Bahrain.

Our mission: work closely with client, architect and consultants to deliver integrated, high-quality and innovative MEP designs for the fullest satisfaction of the client.
